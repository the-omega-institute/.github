## Selected results

### Hiai–Ruskai Conjecture 6.3: exact qubit CQ contraction coefficients

We establish all three exact contraction coefficients for the stated family of qubit channels: dual Wigner–Yanase, geometric and Bogoliubov–Kubo–Mori.

Hiai and Ruskai proposed three optimal bounds in their 2015 preprint. The question asks whether each bound is attained when all admissible states and tangent directions are considered.

This resolves the three metrics in Conjecture 6.3. A formula for every kernel in the broader metric class remains outside this result.

[Read the result](https://www.trureturing.org/conjectures.html#resolved-hiai-ruskai-2015-cq-contraction-coefficients) · [Lean source](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/blob/687878cf845802aba2692eeb304cb960d1306431/D5/S3/Quantum/QuantumChannels/HiaiRuskaiRiemannianContraction.lean) · [Original question](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4936215)

### Erdős–Nicolas reciprocal divisor differences

We prove that for n &gt; 5040, we have Ĥ(n) &lt; τ(n).

No restriction on the order of prime exponents, a divisor-count cutoff, or an unevaluated certificate proposition remains in the theorem's assumptions. The strengthened induction tracks the exceptional integers themselves.

[Read the result](https://www.trureturing.org/conjectures.html#resolved-erdos-nicolas-1989-divisor-difference-sum) · [Lean source](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/blob/687878cf845802aba2692eeb304cb960d1306431/D5/S3/Arith/Divisors/ErdosNicolas/Main.lean) · [Original question](https://oeis.org/A330076)

### Essentially Hermitian off-diagonal blocks

Every complex matrix satisfying the proposed bound for all positive block completions is essentially Hermitian: it is an affine complex transform of a Hermitian matrix.

Bourin and Lee posed this question in 2021. It connects a norm inequality to the geometry of a matrix, and includes an earlier normality question of Hayashi.

The result gives a structural implication for the operator norm. An algorithm producing violating completions and extensions to other norms remain open.

[Read the result](https://www.trureturing.org/conjectures.html#resolved-bourin-lee-2021-essentially-hermitian-block-norm) · [Lean source](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/blob/687878cf845802aba2692eeb304cb960d1306431/D5/S3/Quantum/BlockNorm/EssentiallyHermitian.lean) · [Original question](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0129167X22500094)
